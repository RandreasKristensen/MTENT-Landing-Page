Bring your own setup

Collaborators don't need your DAW or your plugins to join in. They open a link and hear exactly what you hear.

Every version, kept

Changes save in the background, so the good early demo is always there. You never name a file final_v3 again.

Share in one tap

Plugins, automation and samples come along. No bouncing stems, no zipping folders, no lossy previews.

The part nobody enjoys

Five minutes of music, an hour of file admin.

Right now

  • Bounce a mixdown, upload it, wait for a reply.
  • “Do you have Serum?” — they don't.
  • Three folders, four versions, one right answer.
  • The demo everyone loved lives on a dead laptop.
  • One person shares their screen. Everyone else watches.

With gitster

  • The whole project opens in their browser, intact.
  • Your plugin chain runs in the cloud. Nobody buys anything.
  • One project. Every state of it, on a timeline.
  • v4 is still there, still playable, still editable.
  • Everyone works at once. The edits merge.
